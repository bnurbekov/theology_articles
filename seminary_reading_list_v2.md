# Theological Disciplines: A Two-Track Reading List

> A concise, self-directed theological curriculum structured around **A. A. Hodge’s *Outlines of Theology***, extended with additional disciplines for conservative Reformed Baptist ministry.

## How to Use This List

### 1. Two-Track System

* **Track A (Free & Public Domain):** Prioritizes complete, legally accessible texts.
  * **`[PD]`** — Public domain in the United States *(verify specific edition)*.
  * **`[FREE]`** — Full text accessible at no cost *(redistribution rights not established)*.
  * **`[REF]`** — Reference work rather than an introductory textbook.

* **Track B (Modern Recommendations):** Copyrighted textbooks recommended when their added value justifies the cost.
  * *Rule of thumb:* Prefer **one primary textbook** per discipline; treat other works as supplementary.

### 2. Three Stages of Study

1. **Foundations:** Hermeneutics, biblical theology, introductory systematic theology, the 1689 Confession, and biblical languages.
2. **Historical & Doctrinal Depth:** Church history, historical theology, ethics, apologetics, and Particular Baptist covenant theology.
3. **Ministry Preparation:** Preaching, church polity, pastoral counseling, evangelism, missions, and supervised ministry.

*Note: These stages overlap. Begin practical ministry under local church oversight while continuing academic study.*

### 3. Core Study Method (For Every Major Discipline)

1. **Read** the principal work selectively or completely according to its purpose.
2. **Write** a short summary of the author’s main argument.
3. **Identify** the biblical evidence and the principal objections.
4. **Compare** conclusions with the *1689 Second London Baptist Confession* where relevant.
5. **Produce** a written exercise or practical demonstration.
6. **Seek feedback** from a knowledgeable pastor, elder, or teacher.

## Core Disciplines & Reading Tracks

### 1. General Orientation & Apologetics

#### Overview of Disciplines

* **Track A:** `[PD]` A. A. Hodge, *Outlines of Theology* (1878) | `[PD]` Abraham Kuyper, *Encyclopedia of Sacred Theology* (1898)
* **Track B:** No additional introductory textbook required initially.

> *Use Hodge and Kuyper for broad orientation, not as substitutes for individual discipline study.*

#### Apologetics

* **Track A:** `[PD]` Joseph Butler, *The Analogy of Religion*
* **Track B:** Cornelius Van Til, *The Defense of the Faith*
* **Exercise:** Write a short defense of the Christian faith and explain how you would respond to one major objection.

---

### 2. Exegetical Theology

#### Biblical Languages

* **Greek Grammar:**
  * **Track A:** `[REF]` A. T. Robertson, *A Grammar of the Greek New Testament in the Light of Historical Research*
  * **Track B:** William D. Mounce, *Basics of Biblical Greek* | Daniel B. Wallace, *Greek Grammar Beyond the Basics*

* **Hebrew Grammar:**
  * **Track A:** `[REF]` Gesenius, *Hebrew Grammar* (ed. Kautzsch & Cowley)
  * **Track B:** Gary D. Pratico & Miles V. Van Pelt, *Basics of Biblical Hebrew Grammar*

* **Method:** Start with a beginner textbook $\rightarrow$ Learn vocabulary/paradigms $\rightarrow$ Translate biblical passages $\rightarrow$ Use advanced grammars for complex constructions.

#### Hermeneutics & Biblical Introduction

* **General Hermeneutics:**
  * **Track A:** `[PD]` Milton S. Terry, *Biblical Hermeneutics* | `[PD]` Frederick Scrivener, *A Plain Introduction to the Criticism of the New Testament*
  * **Track B:** No additional textbook required initially.
  * **Exercise:** Interpret a passage by identifying its literary context, historical setting, structure, argument, and canonical relationship before consulting commentaries.

* **Special Introduction:**
  * **Track A:** `[PD]` B. F. Westcott, *An Introduction to the Study of the Gospels* | `[PD]` C. F. Keil, *Manual of Historico-Critical Introduction to the Canonical Scriptures of the OT*
  * **Track B (NT):** D. A. Carson & Douglas J. Moo, *An Introduction to the New Testament*
  * **Track B (OT):** Edward J. Young, *An Introduction to the Old Testament*

#### Exegesis & Biblical Theology

* **Commentaries:**
  * **Track A:** `[PD]` John Calvin, *Commentaries*
  * **Track B:** Choose a reliable modern commentary for the biblical book being studied rather than purchasing a complete set immediately.

* **Biblical Theology:**
  * **Track A:** `[PD]` Gustave Friedrich Oehler, *Theology of the Old Testament* | `[PD]` Bernhard Weiss, *Biblical Theology of the New Testament*
  * **Track B:** Geerhardus Vos, *Biblical Theology: Old and New Testaments*
  * **Exercise:** Trace one theme (e.g., *covenant, kingdom, temple, priesthood*) from its OT foundations through fulfillment in Christ.

* **Ecclesiological Biblical Theology:**
  * **Track A:** `[PD]` James Bannerman, *The Church of Christ*
  * **Primary Texts:** Gen 12, 17; Ex 19; Matt 16, 28; Acts 2, 15; Eph 2, 5; 1 Tim 3; Rev 21–22.
  * **Confessional Supplement:** 1689 LBCF, Chapter 26.
  * *Note:* Bannerman is Presbyterian; evaluate his arguments on covenant continuity and ordinances against Particular Baptist convictions.

---

### 3. Systematic Theology & Ethics

#### Systematic Theology

* **Track A:** `[PD]` Charles Hodge, *Systematic Theology* (3 Vols) | `[PD]` John Calvin, *Institutes of the Christian Religion*
* **Track B:** Louis Berkhof, *Systematic Theology*
* **Confessional Supplement:** 1689 Second London Baptist Confession.
* **Exercise:** Write a concise doctrinal statement on one subject, citing relevant Scripture and comparing your conclusion with the 1689 Confession.

#### Ethics

* **Track A:** `[PD]` Archibald Alexander, *Outlines of Moral Science* | `[PD]` Joseph Butler, *Dissertation on the Nature of Virtue*
* **Track B:** John Murray, *Principles of Conduct* | John Frame, *The Doctrine of the Christian Life*

---

### 4. Historical Theology

* **General Church History:**
  * **Track A:** `[PD]` Philip Schaff, *History of the Christian Church* | `[PD]` W. G. T. Shedd, *History of Christian Doctrine* | `[PD]` Philip Schaff, *The Creeds of Christendom*
  * **Track B:** No additional general survey required initially.

* **Reformation & Particular Baptist History:**
  * **Track A:** Primary documents of the English Reformation & 1689 Confession | `[PD]` Nehemiah Coxe, *A Discourse of the Covenants*
  * **Track B:** No additional textbook required initially.
  * **Focus:** Particular Baptist covenant theology, confessional subscription, believer's baptism, and local church autonomy.
