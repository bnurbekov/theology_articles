We have all sinned and fallen short of the glory of God.
God requires us to be perfectly holy as He is holy, to be perfectly righteous as He is righteous. He requires us to love our neighbor as ourselves and Him with all our heart, with all our mind, and with all our strength.
But we have all sinned, and our own righteousness is like filthy rags. We have lied before, stolen things before, been angry before, lusted after women before. The Bible says if you hate your brother, you are a murderer. If you look at a woman with lustful intent, then you've already committed adultery with her in your heart.
The punishment for all our sins is eternal punishment, because our sin violates the infinite holiness of God. It incurs the infinite anger, the infinite wrath of God.
Hell is described as the lake of fire, a place where the fire is never quenched and the worm never dies, a place where there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth, a place of eternal torment where there will be no rest for the worshippers of the beast. The worm that eats dead people dies on earth, but in hell it never dies.
Hell is eternal death. You have light here on earth, you can rest, but you have no light in hell. You have no rest from the torment that goes on there. And we all deserve to go there for our sins.
But the good news is that God so loved the world that He gave His only Son, so that whoever believes in Him shall not perish but shall have eternal life (John 3:16).
God, in His mercy and love, sent His only Son, Jesus Christ, to live a life of perfect righteousness that you and I could never live, and to die on the cross the death that we deserve. Jesus Christ died on the cross, and He was resurrected on the third day, defeating death, Satan, and sin.
The Bible says, "For our sake He made Him who knew no sin to be sin, so that in Him we might become the righteousness of God" (2 Corinthians 5:21).
This verse describes the great exchange. Jesus Christ took the sins of all the believers to the cross and received their punishment, the curse that they deserved, the death. And in exchange, the righteousness of Jesus Christ is counted toward all the believers so that they can have eternal life, the blessing that Jesus Christ deserves.
If you confess with your mouth that Jesus is Lord and believe in your heart that God raised Him from the dead, then you will be saved (Romans 10:9).
In other words, if you repent and believe in Jesus Christ as the God-man, who died for your sins on the cross, who was resurrected on the third day, who lived a life of perfect righteousness in your place, then you will have eternal life.
God is one being, but He exists in three distinct persons: the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit. Jesus Christ is the Son of God; He is the one who came down from heaven to save us by dying for our sins on the cross. Jesus Christ is the God-man, and only He was able to save us by taking the infinite punishment, the infinite wrath of God, on the cross. Only the God-man was able to take upon Himself the punishment we deserve in hell so that we could be saved.
Do not rely on your good works.
Our good works are like filthy rags (Isaiah 64:6). The Bible says, "By grace you have been saved, through faith, and that not of yourselves; it is the free gift of God, not a result of works, so that no one may boast" (Ephesians 2:8–9).
If we were saved by good works, then we could boast in the sight of God. We could say, "God, I've done X, Y, and Z, and now I'm saved because of my own merit."
That is why salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone, according to the Scriptures alone, to the glory of God alone.
