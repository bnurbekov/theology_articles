# Theological Disciplines: A Two-Track Reading List

Disciplines follow A. A. Hodge's *Outlines of Theology* (1878 ed., ch. 1), plus Ethics. Hodge's own text is public domain.

**Track A – Public domain.** Free to read, copy, and host (US). Use scans of the original editions, not modern reprints, which may add protected material.
**Track B – Modern recommended.** Copyrighted. List and link them; do not copy or host the text.

> Track B picks rest on seminary reviews and forum recommendations, not an authoritative ranking. Items marked "not researched" were not searched.

---

## Overview of the disciplines
- **Track A:** Hodge, *Outlines of Theology* (1878); Schaff, *Theological Propaedeutic* (the best single study guide I found) – https://commons.ptsem.edu/id/theologicalpropd03scha ; Weidner, *Theological Encyclopaedia and Methodology*; Kuyper, *Encyclopedia of Sacred Theology* (1898, Reformed) – https://commons.ptsem.edu/id/encyclopediaofsa00kuyp/low-bandwidth
- **Track B:** not researched.

## Apologetics
- **Track A:** Butler, *The Analogy of Religion* – https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/53346
- **Track B:** not researched.

## Exegetical theology

### Biblical languages
**Greek grammar**
- **Track A:** Robertson, *A Grammar of the Greek New Testament* (1914; reference-size, about 1,400 pages) – https://commons.ptsem.edu/id/grammarofgreekne00robeuoft
- **Track B:** Mounce, *Basics of Biblical Greek*; Wallace, *Greek Grammar Beyond the Basics* (intermediate).

**Hebrew grammar**
- **Track A:** Gesenius' *Hebrew Grammar*, Kautzsch–Cowley (1910; reference grammar) – https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Gesenius'_Hebrew_Grammar
- **Track B:** Pratico and Van Pelt, *Basics of Biblical Hebrew Grammar*.

### General introduction
- **Track A:** Terry, *Biblical Hermeneutics* – https://commons.ptsem.edu/id/biblicalhermeneu00terr ; Scrivener, *A Plain Introduction to the Criticism of the New Testament* (textual criticism) – Vol. I https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/36548 , Vol. II https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/36549
- **Track B:** not researched.

### Special introduction
- **Track A (New Testament):** Zahn, *Introduction to the New Testament* (German scholar; described as defending authenticity) – https://catalog.hathitrust.org/Record/008686689
- **Track A (Old Testament):** Keil, *Manual of Historico-Critical Introduction to the Canonical Scriptures of the Old Testament* (described as conservative; supports Mosaic authorship) – https://babel.hathitrust.org/cgi/pt?id=nnc1.cr00252107&view=2up&seq=10&size=125
- **Track B (New Testament):** Carson and Moo (with Morris in the original edition); reviewed as thoroughly evangelical and of conservative conviction.
- **Track B (Old Testament):** E. J. Young, *An Introduction to the Old Testament* (conservative). Dillard and Longman is widely used, but one forum voice says it makes limited compromises with the critical tradition on authorship of some books.

### Exegesis (commentary)
- **Track A:** Calvin's *Commentaries* (Calvin Translation Society edition; covers most books of the Bible) – https://ccel.org/c/calvin/commentaries/home.html
- **Track B:** not researched.

### Biblical theology
- **Track A:** Oehler, *Theology of the Old Testament* – https://ccel.org/ccel/oehler/ot_theol ; Weiss, *Biblical Theology of the New Testament* (German evangelical scholar; opposed the Tübingen school; not Reformed) – https://commons.ptsem.edu/id/biblicaltheology01weis and https://commons.ptsem.edu/id/biblicaltheology13weis
- **Track B:** Vos, *Biblical Theology: Old and New Testaments* (Reformed classic covering both Testaments; not a beginner's book).

## Systematic theology
- **Track A:** Hodge, *Systematic Theology* (3 vols.) – https://ccel.org/ccel/hodge/theology1 (also theology2, theology3). Calvin's *Institutes* (Beveridge translation) is on CCEL.
- **Track B:** Berkhof, *Systematic Theology*. Praised for concision and clarity; one reviewer notes it leans toward proof-texting and is largely a summary of Bavinck. A free legal ebook is offered via BiblicalTraining.org (permission-based, not public domain): https://www.thegospelcoalition.org/blogs/justin-taylor/louis-berkhofs-systematic-theology-now-free-as-an-ebook/

## Ethics
- **Track A:** Alexander, *Outlines of Moral Science* (theistic approach) – https://ccel.org/ccel/alexander_a/outlines ; Butler's *Dissertation on the Nature of Virtue*, bound with the *Analogy*.
- **Track B:** Murray, *Principles of Conduct*; Frame, *The Doctrine of the Christian Life*. Evidence is thin (forum recommendations).

## Practical theology
- **Track A:** Broadus, *On the Preparation and Delivery of Sermons* (use the 1870 original or the 1897 Dargan edition, not later revisions); Baxter, *The Reformed Pastor* – https://cblibrary.org/pastoral_aids/pastoral_aids.htm
- **Track B:** Stott, *Between Two Worlds*; Robinson, *Biblical Preaching* (called the standard seminary textbook on expository sermons).

## Historical theology
- **Track A:** Schaff, *History of the Christian Church* – https://ccel.org/ccel/schaff (Schaff's Mercersburg theology was charged with being too pro-Catholic and he was acquitted; read his interpretive judgments with care.) Shedd, *History of Christian Doctrine* (Augustino-Calvinistic) – https://dl.atla.com/concern/works/sf268g73h?locale=en
- **Track B:** not researched.
